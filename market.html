THE PROBLEM

Owning a Mediterranean luxury villa today is either a full illiquid commitment, or a purely financial product with no lifestyle value.

01

HIGH COST OF ENTRY

A single Mediterranean villa costs €2–10m+ - out of reach for most affluent buyers, even those who could afford meaningful exposure to the asset class.

02

CHRONIC UNDERUSE

Second homes are empty most of the year. Owners pay all the costs but only use them for a few weeks.

03

NO MIDDLE GROUND TODAY

Usage-led co-ownership (Pacaso, August, MYNE, Vivla) gives you a holiday home, not a liquid investment. Investment platforms (Stake, Lofty, Bricksave) give you yield, never a do operational hospitality in luxury properties.

WHO FEELS THIS

ROYA serves two groups: HNW property owners who want to sell all or part of their villa, and people with smaller savings, such as salaried employees, who want to invest in a share but cannot afford a whole villa.
