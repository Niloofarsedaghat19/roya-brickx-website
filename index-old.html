HOW IT WORKS

REAL PROPERTY. SHARED INCOME. MADE SIMPLE.

Buy tokens that represent your share in a property investment, while Roya manages the asset and distributes available cash to token holders.

01

CHOOSE YOUR ASSET

Review the property, its business plan and risks, then choose how much to invest in tokens.

02

ROYA OPERATES IT

Guests pay to stay while Roya manages bookings, maintenance and costs to improve operating profits.

03

RECEIVE YOUR SHARE

After costs, fees, debt payments and reserves, available cash is shared with token holders under the investment terms.

04

MANAGE IN THE APP

Track performance and distributions, vote where your terms allow, and explore selling options when you want to exit.
